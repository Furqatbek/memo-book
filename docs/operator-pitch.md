# Tour Operator Pitch Sheet — Phase 0

> **Purpose:** script + discovery questions for the five in-person operator meetings (brief §12, Phase 0). Goal by **15 September 2026**: **2 signed operator agreements or 5 prepaid retail orders.** Agencies must be signed in **August** to catch the September–October season.
>
> Bring: one physical A5 sample book (non-negotiable — the sample **is** the pitch), this sheet, and a one-page terms draft.

---

## The 60-second pitch

> Your clients end a once-in-a-lifetime trip with 1,400 photos they will never look at.
> We turn those photos into a hand-designed A5 hardcover book — lay-flat binding, silk paper, presentation box, Uzbek-motif cover — printed here in Tashkent.
> The client sends photos over WhatsApp; we design it; they approve a proof; your guide hands them a boxed book at the farewell dinner.
> Retail is $149. Your price is $99 — you keep $50 per book, or bundle it into your premium package. Zero work for your team: we collect the photos, design, print and deliver.

**Why it helps the operator (their job-to-be-done, brief §5):** end the tour on a note the client remembers, tells friends about, and mentions in the review. It photographs well in the farewell-dinner moment.

## Terms to open with (provisional — brief §6, §10)

| Term | Position | Walk-away line |
|---|---|---|
| Wholesale price | **$99** (retail $149) | Test $79–99 if attach-rate argument is strong; **do not** go below $79 before A5 COGS is re-quoted |
| Settlement | **Prepaid, or operator collects and settles weekly** | **Never** 30–60 day terms. Non-negotiable (Risk 5: printer wants cash up front; we do not carry receivables) |
| Branding | Co-brand by default; white-label possible | White-label at a higher wholesale price |
| Sales model | Their choice: paid upsell OR bundled into premium package | — |
| Turnaround | Proof 72h from photos; print 1–2 days after approval | Client must start photos ≥4–5 days before departure for hand delivery |
| Defects | Free reprint at our cost | — |

---

## Discovery questions (Open Question #5 — ask, don't assume)

1. What commission / margin makes this worth putting in front of every group? (Listen for whether $50/book lands.)
2. Would you sell it as a **paid upsell** or bake it into a **premium package** price?
3. Who should collect the money — you or us? What payment methods do your clients actually use?
4. Co-branded ("in partnership with [Agency]") or white-label under your own name?
5. When in the tour is the right moment to offer it — booking, mid-tour, or final days?
6. Do your groups have a photographer whose shots we could start from?
7. **A5 price check (Open Question #4/§6):** hand them the sample. "What would your clients pay for this without thinking twice?" — decide $149 vs $89–119 from their answers, not from a desk.
8. **Disintermediation check (Risk 4):** "What would make you keep working with us rather than going straight to a printer?" Ask it directly; the answer is the moat, or the warning.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Our clients won't pay $149" | "What would they pay without deliberating? We can price the A5 at what converts — that's exactly what we're here to learn." (The $89–119 range is pre-authorised by the brief.) |
| "Too much work for guides" | "The guide hands over a box. Everything else — photo collection, design, revisions, printing — is us, over WhatsApp, directly with the client." |
| "What if the book is late?" | "If we miss the departure window, we ship it to their home at our cost for the shipping difference, and you still get your margin." |
| "What if it's defective?" | "Free reprint, our cost, no argument. Lay-flat binding exists precisely because ordinary photobook spines crack." |
| "Can we see quality first?" | Hand them the sample. Offer to make **one free book from a real past tour's photos** as a trial for their premium clients. |
