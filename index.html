How it works

From camera roll to hardcover, in four steps

No design skills needed. The editor runs in your browser — nothing to install.

  1. Start with a blank book

    Open the editor and get an empty book — your pages, your rules. No forced templates.

  2. Upload your photos

    One at a time or all at once — bring in your whole trip in a single bulk upload.

  3. Design pages & cover

    Place photos exactly where you want them, or let auto-layout fill the pages for you and rearrange from there. Add text anywhere. Design the cover to match.

  4. Pay, we print & ship

    When you're happy, pay online. Your finished design goes to print exactly as you made it, and the hardcover book is delivered to your door.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need design skills?

No. Auto-layout fills your pages in one click, and everything stays editable — move, resize and swap until it looks right. Most people finish a book in an evening.

Some of my photos are blurry. Can you fix them?

Yes — we use AI enhancement to improve blurry or muddy photos before printing, so your pages come out looking their best.

How do I pay?

Online, in Uzbek so'm, through local payment systems. You pay only when you order the printed book — designing is free.

How long until my book arrives?

Around 30 days from payment to delivery. We print your design, check it page by page, and ship it to your address.

Will the printed book match what I designed?

Yes — what you see in the editor is what we print. You design every page yourself, so there are no surprises.

What if my book arrives damaged or misprinted?

We reprint it free of charge — no argument. Every book also ships in protective packaging.
