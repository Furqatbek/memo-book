How it works

No app, no editor, no drag-and-drop. One conversation, four steps.

  1. Send 40–80 photos

    Straight from your phone, via WhatsApp, Telegram or an upload link. Don't sort them — that's our job.

  2. We design your book

    A designer builds the story by hand: chronology, pacing, captions and a cover made for your trip.

  3. Proof in 72 hours

    You get a full digital preview. Two rounds of revisions are included — swap photos, fix names, change the cover.

  4. Printed & delivered

    Printed in Tashkent, quality-checked page by page, handed to you before departure or shipped to your home.

The book itself

Built to be handled, pressed flat on tables, and passed around a dinner party — for years.

A5 hardcover

14.8 × 21 cm, 20–60 pages. Sized for a bookshelf and a carry-on. It reads as a book, not an album.

Lay-flat binding

Every spread opens completely flat, so panoramas run across both pages without vanishing into the spine. No cracked glue, no falling pages — the failure that plagues ordinary photo books.

200gsm silk paper

Thick, silk-coated stock with deep, accurate colour. Pages that feel like a gallery catalogue, not a brochure.

Presentation box

A matte-laminated cover in a protective presentation box. It survives the flight home and opens like a gift — because it is one.

Uzbek by design

Cover collections drawn from suzani embroidery and the tilework of Registan, Bukhara and Khiva, with patterned endpapers. A souvenir of this country — not a template from a server farm.

Captions in your language

English, Russian or Uzbek — or all three. Place names spelled correctly, dates in order, your inside jokes where they belong.

Questions travellers ask

My photos are just phone photos. Are they good enough?

Almost always, yes. Modern phone photos print beautifully at A5 size. Every photo is checked by a person before layout — if one genuinely won't print well, we tell you and ask for an alternative rather than printing something you'd regret. We don't use AI "enhancement" that invents detail.

I fly home in three days. Is that enough time?

It's tight, but your book gets made either way. Send photos today and your proof arrives within 72 hours; if printing can't finish before your flight, we ship the finished book to your home — you approve the proof from anywhere. Sending photos five or more days before departure is the safe way to fly home with the book in your bag.

I've already flown home. Too late?

Not at all. Send your photos from anywhere; we design and print in Tashkent and ship internationally. Shipping cost and time are quoted up front, before you pay.

Will it survive the flight and the mail?

The book ships inside a rigid presentation box, and the lay-flat binding has no brittle glued spine to crack. If a book ever arrives damaged or with a printing defect, we reprint it at our cost — no argument.

Can I see a real copy before ordering?

If you're in Tashkent, message us and we'll show you a sample in person. We're also happy to send close-up photos and a page-turn video of a finished book.

How do I pay?

In US dollars — we confirm the payment method when you order: card, or through your tour operator if they offer Silkbound books. You approve the final proof before printing, and nothing is printed until you're happy.
